# Digital Twin initiatives of CEOS agencies

A comparative synthesis of agency approaches. 

Core activity : Mapping of DT approaches CEOS agencies.  Analysis of the different components and approaches taken by CEOS agencies in DTs. Commonalities and where they differ. 

EO Digital Twins in development (in alphabetic order):
- CNES : DT factory
- ESA : Destination Earth and Hydrology twin?
- ISRO : Geospatial DT and applications
- ICHEC : small scale lake DT 
- NASA: Natural Hazards DT + more?
- UK: PML’s Marine DT, ATI? And Cambridge’s construction DT. UK's EODH containing several elements of a DT 
- Any others?
